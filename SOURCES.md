## Research Notes

### Primary vs. Secondary Sources

- **Primary sources**: Original Qing dynasty texts, historical records, early films
- **Secondary sources**: Academic analysis, modern interpretations, contemporary media

### Cultural Sensitivity

When researching jiangshi, be mindful that:
- These stories are part of living cultural traditions
- Funeral and death customs are deeply personal and sacred
- Traditional beliefs deserve respectful treatment
- Modern pop culture interpretations differ from traditional folklore

### Language Considerations

- Jiangshi = 僵尸 (Simplified Chinese) / 殭屍 (Traditional Chinese)
- Multiple romanization systems exist (Pinyin: jiāngshī; Wade-Giles: chiang-shih)
- Japanese and Korean pronunciations differ (Kyonshī, Gangshi)
